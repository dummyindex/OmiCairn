## Generic guidance (scope)

- Profile the data first. If a column named in RESEARCH.md is missing,
  stop and report it in `data_profile.md` rather than improvising.
- A numeric success threshold is mandatory. "The model does well" is
  not a hypothesis; "the primary metric exceeds the baseline by ≥X
  with a 95% CI excluding 0" is.
- Negative results are valuable. Frame what a negative result would
  teach the field, not just what a positive one would.
